# V2.6 — Field runner access

**What a runner (role `field_staff`) can do**
- Open only: My day (`/staff/pickups`), Fleet map (cars not out on rental only), Report, My profile. Every other `/staff/*` page redirects to My day. Enforced in `lib/supabase/middleware.ts` using `lib/runner-access.ts`.
- My day shows only rentals assigned to them (`rental.assigned_runner_id`). Admin assigns on each pickup/dropoff card.
- Sees renter name, phone, car, time, agreement signed or not, and whether the card was charged (no amounts, no rates).
- Cannot hand over keys until the agreement is signed (server-checked in `confirmPickup`).
- Cannot schedule rentals, change appointments, drop-off dates, insurance, or record payments (`OFFICE_ONLY` guard in `rental-actions.ts`; `collect_payment` removed from the role in migration 0094).
- Report page: log a car problem (incident) or a toll/ticket (insert-only via new permission `log_field_report`).

**Setup**
1. Run `0094_runner_access.sql` in Supabase.
2. Team → invite the runner with the field staff role.
3. Pickups & dropoffs → set "Assigned runner" on each rental.

**Known limits**
- Database read access is still tenant-wide for runners. The page lock and query scoping are in the app. Tightening RLS per role is a follow-up before adding less-trusted runners.
- The lock is by page. Server actions on office pages remain protected by their database permission guards.
